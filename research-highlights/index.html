Do Robust LVLMs Hallucinate More?

Uncovering robustness-induced hallucination in large vision-language models

Adversarial robustness is usually treated as a pure win. We examine whether robustly fine-tuned LVLMs pay for it in faithfulness, and characterize the hallucination that robustness training can introduce.

RobustChart360 EMNLP 2026 · Industry Track

From Benchmarks to Real-World Use

Evaluating the robustness of vision-language models for chart understanding

We test 14 VLMs on 2,505 charts across six real-world robustness dimensions. Accuracy drops by up to 31% under caption mismatches and by over 50% at severe low resolution, and models over-rely on text when visual and textual signals conflict.

Unsupervised Siamese adversarial fine-tuning for robust, semantically rich VLMs

A Siamese fine-tuning scheme with a cosine-similarity objective and symmetric stop-gradient that makes CLIP encoders robust without large batches or momentum encoders, while keeping the semantic detail VLMs need.

Secure yet Fragile Scientific Reports · 2026

Secure yet Fragile

Adversarial vulnerabilities of federated vision–language models in medical AI

A systematic audit of CLIP-based medical VLMs trained with FedAvg, FedProx, FedPer and FedBN, under four attacks on three MedMNIST datasets. Client-level perturbations propagate through aggregation; the test-time defense CLIPure gives the most consistent recovery.

Trustworthy AVs CVPR 2025 · Workshops

Towards Trustworthy Autonomous Vehicles

Vision-language models under targeted and untargeted adversarial attacks

We stress-test CLIP, TeCoA, FARE and Sim-CLIP encoders inside driving VLMs on LingoQA, NuScenes-QA, ROADWork and CoVLA, using richly re-annotated scenes. Sim-CLIP is the most robust while keeping accuracy high.

Projects in progress

Active projects across vision-language models, generative AI and safety-critical applications.

Ongoing

RoadSentry: AI-Powered Roadside Construction Safety Intelligence

An AI safety platform for roadside work zones that combines computer vision, multi-object tracking and vision-language models to monitor workers, vehicles and equipment, flag hazards and near-misses, and raise real-time alerts. A spatial safety layer reasons about restricted-zone entry, worker–equipment proximity and movement, while a central dashboard surfaces AI-generated incident summaries, risk levels, visual evidence and safety trends.

Ongoing

Developing Robust and Reliable Vision Language Models

Vision-language models that accurately interpret visual content and generate meaningful, contextually grounded descriptions, improving both visual understanding and text generation for captioning, visual question answering and other tasks that require deep joint reasoning over images and text.

Ongoing

Making Multimodal LLMs Efficient during Runtime

Token pruning strategies for Large Vision-Language Models that identify and discard redundant visual tokens across the pipeline, cutting compute and memory while preserving visual reasoning and question answering, for deployment on resource-constrained edge devices.

Ongoing

Self-Correcting Multimodal LLMs

Techniques that let an LVLM notice when it starts hallucinating and correct itself, producing accurate, grounded responses without external intervention or human feedback, to make LVLMs more reliable in real-world deployment.

Ongoing

Fine-Grained Diffusion for Realistic Image Generation

Improving diffusion models so that fine-grained semantics in a prompt (object relationships, spatial arrangements, subtle visual cues) are faithfully reflected in the generated image, enabling precise, controllable synthesis for autonomous driving, simulation and scientific visualization.
