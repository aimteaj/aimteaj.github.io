🌊 <span style="color:#00796b; font-weight:800;">Received a Vertically Integrated Projects (VIP) Grant as PI for CoastSight!</span>

Excited to receive a <strong>FAU Vertically Integrated Projects (VIP) Grant</strong> for our project, <span style="color:#1565c0; font-weight:700;">“CoastSight: Generative and Vision-Language AI for Disaster Intelligence.”</span>

Led by <strong>Dr. Ahmed Imteaj, Dr. Stephanie Wakefield, and Dr. Jason O. Hallstrom</strong>, CoastSight explores <span style="color:#6a1b9a; font-weight:700;">Generative AI and Vision-Language Models</span> for disaster intelligence and coastal resilience while engaging students in interdisciplinary, hands-on research.
