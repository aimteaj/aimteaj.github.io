Awarded an **NSF NAIRR Pilot Classroom allocation** for "Hands-on AI Fundamentals and Modern Generative AI Education Using Classroom-Scale GPU and LLM Resources." About 95 students in CAP 4630: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence will use a shared GPU environment on the Prototype National Research Platform in Spring 2027 for hands-on work with deep learning, LLMs and vision-language models. The proposal was rated Excellent and approved in full.
