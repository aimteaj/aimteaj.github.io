I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Florida Atlantic University, Boca Raton. I serve as a Faculty Fellow at the Institute for Smarter Cities, Spaces, and Health (I-SMART), am a senior member of the Center for Connected Autonomy and Artificial Intelligence (CA-AI), and founded and direct the Secure Prediction, Edge AI and Multimodal LLM Lab (SPEED Lab).

My research focuses on building robust, secure, efficient, and trustworthy AI systems, with an emphasis on large vision-language models, agentic AI, federated learning, multimodal LLMs, and edge intelligence. I am particularly interested in the control theory of LLMs and distributed intelligence, and in practical deployment for autonomous transportation, smart cities, surveillance, operational intelligence for mission-critical situations, underwater robotics, agriculture and healthcare. Our work appears in leading venues including NeurIPS, CVPR, ACL, EMNLP, ICCV, AAAI, IEEE BigData, ICDCS, Nature Scientific Reports and IEEE Transactions.

🧬 Our work on secure and trustworthy healthcare AI has been published in Nature Scientific Reports!

Our paper titled, “Secure yet fragile: adversarial vulnerabilities of federated vision–language models in medical AI” examines federated Vision-Language Models for medical AI and demonstrates how adversarial perturbations introduced at one participating site can propagate through collaborative training and substantially degrade the shared model’s performance.
